Method & evidence

A small model.
A bounded conclusion.

Probabilities describe recorded outcomes in this cohort, not intrinsic talent or the causal effect of choosing a bowler.

What the profiles contain

Dot = zero total runs on a legal delivery. Boundary = a batter four or six, excluding non-boundary flags. Other = the remaining legal deliveries. Illegal deliveries are counted separately. These measures are not economy or credited wickets.

Held-out 2026 check

Retrospective evaluation, separate from the 2023–2025 profiles. Lower Brier is better; it is not an accuracy percentage.

Limits, coverage and provenance
    How to read the evidence states

    Limited evidence: fewer than 60 legal deliveries or five matches. These are operational thresholds, not a statistical guarantee. No history means a phase fallback, not a personal estimate.

    Uncertain: a reference contrast spans zero, or its direction changes after removing one match or the dominant venue. Reviewable: passes those stated checks; it still does not recommend a lineup. Unavailable checks remain visible.

    Intervals are conditional on the fixed model and cohort. They are not probabilities of player superiority. Inspecting multiple profiles is exploratory.
